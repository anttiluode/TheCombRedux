Training and data

The 16D input and rotation weights are fixed. Offline supervised training learns a four-class readout and two comb operators from independently generated simulated episodes. The frozen model is then evaluated on new seeded streams; hidden truth is used only to score the experiment.

What this is / isn't

The workspace is a learned four-class representation, not Anthropic's Jacobian-lens J-space. The temporal loop is a fixed nonlinear reservoir, not a learned cortical wave or a self. The operator bank learns two elementary transformations, not open-ended program discovery. Ablations and comparisons are exploratory until preregistered.

Experimental interpretation

Necessary interventions: (1) remove the entire recurrent state; (2) remove coordinates 2–15; (3) cut its workspace read. Compare probability and Brier score, not only accuracy at unstable 50% ties. Repeated matching seeds keep the observation schedule identical. A 4-hypothesis Bayesian filter is the stronger known-model reference—not part of the learner.

A possible result is that a 16D rotating reservoir loses to a simple Bayesian filter. That would be useful: it would reveal an addressing or training failure, rather than prove that circulation is special. The structural twist and genuine J-space read/write remain future hypotheses.
